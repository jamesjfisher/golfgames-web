Match Play

Match Play is decided hole by hole. Win a hole and you go one up; the player who wins more holes wins the match.

Players2 or more, playing head-to-head matches
MatchesWith 2 players, the match is set for you. With 3 or more, you choose who plays who.
ScoringGross (default) or net, with an optional handicap allowance
MoneyMatch worth: Free, $5, $10 or $20 a match

Setting it up

On the New Round Format step, add Match Play ("Hole-by-hole winner") and open its options.

  • Who plays who: tap two players to pair them.
    • You can set up several matches in one round, and one player can be in more than one.
    • The screen shows what that could cost: "N matches at $X each — up to $Y across the round for a player in all of them."
    • Anyone not in a match still plays the round and keeps a full stroke play card.
    • With 3 or more players the round won't start until at least one match is set.
  • Scoring: Gross ("Raw scores decide each hole.") or Net ("Handicap strokes are applied per hole, then the hole is decided.").
  • Handicap allowance (Net only): 100%, 90%, 80% or Custom.
  • Match worth: the same amount applies to every match, in whole dollars. Free is the default.

How it's scored

  • Each hole: the lower score wins it, and equal scores halve it.
  • The running score counts holes won minus holes lost: AS (all square), 2 UP or 2 DN.
  • Dormie: you're as many holes up as there are left to play.
  • The match ends early once one player is more holes up than there are holes left. The result is written as holes up and holes left: 4&3 means 4 up with 3 to play.
  • A match decided on the last hole reads 1 UP (or 2 UP). A match level after the last hole is All Square, a halved match.
  • Net matches: each player gets their own playing handicap (course handicap × allowance), spread over the holes by stroke index. The hole is decided on the net scores.

Once a match is decided, the scorecard's Match total stays at the result (for example 4&3), while the hole cells keep showing the running count if you play on.

Worked example

Alex plays Sam, gross, with Match worth set to $10.

Hole1234
Par4435
Alex4534
Sam5525
Match (Alex)1 UP1 UPAS1 UP

Alex wins holes 1 and 4, Sam wins hole 3, and hole 2 is halved: Alex is 1 UP after four. Alex goes on to win holes 6, 9 and 10, and after hole 15 is 4 up with 3 to play. The match ends there: Alex wins 4&3, and Sam owes Alex $10.

In the app

Partway through a round, the scorecard shows everyone's holes. Tap Match Play under YOUR GAMES to see each match and its standing. These screens are from a different round than the worked example above.

Settling up

  • A decided match: the loser owes the winner the match amount.
  • Several matches: a player in more than one gets the total of all of them.
  • No payout: a halved match pays nothing, and so does a match that's still undecided when a round is abandoned. The leader isn't awarded it.
  • No presses: Match Play has none. For presses, play Nassau.

Good to know

  • Picking up loses the hole. If both players pick up, the hole is halved.
  • An unscored hole (one player has no score yet) is skipped until both scores are in.
  • 9-hole rounds play a 9-hole match, and results like 3&2 work the same way.
  • When a match is decided, you'll see "Name wins 4&3". If it's the round's only game, you can go to the Round Summary or keep playing.
