Four-Ball

Four-Ball is two teams of two. On every hole each team counts its better ball, the lower of the two partners' scores. Play it as stroke play (lowest team total) or match play (holes won).

Players4 in the game, as two teams of two. The round can have more players; they sit out of the Four-Ball.
Scoring styleStroke Play (default) or Match Play
ScoringGross (default) or net, with an optional handicap allowance
MoneyMatch Play only: Match worth, Free, $5, $10 or $20 per player. Stroke Play Four-Ball is played for nothing.

Setting it up

On the Format step, add Four-Ball ("Best ball per team of 2"). It needs at least four players in the round.

  • Teams: shows "Not set" until you pair the players. Tap swap to rearrange. The round won't start until the teams are set.
    • Teams are named after their players, for example "Alex & Jordan".
    • With more than four players, you pick the four and who partners who. Everyone else is shown as "sits out".
  • Scoring style: Stroke Play ("Each team's better ball counts; lowest total wins.") or Match Play ("Each team's better ball counts; won hole by hole.").
  • Scoring: Gross or Net. With net, "Handicap strokes apply to each partner's ball, then the team takes the better one."
  • Handicap allowance: 100%, 90%, 80% or Custom.
  • Match worth (Match Play only): "$X per player — so the losing side pays $2X between them."

How it's scored

  • Team score on a hole: the lower of the two partners' scores. With net, each partner's score is netted first, then the better one counts.
  • Stroke Play: each team's better balls are added up, and the lower total wins. Equal totals are a tie. The winner is decided once both teams have a score on every hole.
  • Match Play: the lower team ball wins the hole, and equal balls halve it. The match reads AS, 2 UP or 2 DN, can end early (3&2), and is All Square if level at the end.

In the app

Partway through a round, the scorecard shows everyone's holes. Tap Four-Ball under YOUR GAMES to see holes won by each team, and the match standing. These screens are from a different round than the worked example above.

Settling up

  • Only a decided Match Play Four-Ball pays. Each winning player gets the Match worth and each losing player pays it. At $10, Sam and Casey would pay $10 each, $20 between them.
  • No payout: an All Square match pays nothing, and neither does an unfinished one. A Stroke Play Four-Ball shows a winner, but involves no money.
  • Players sitting out aren't part of the settle-up.

Good to know

  • Picking up: if one partner picks up, the team simply uses the other partner's ball.
    • If both pick up, the team loses the hole in Match Play.
    • In Stroke Play, both picking up is counted as a net double bogey (par + 2).
    • If neither team has a ball, the hole is halved.
  • A hole that's waiting on a score isn't decided until the team's scores are in.
  • Players added during the round don't join the Four-Ball teams.
  • Can't be combined with Wolf, Banker, 6-6-6 or Vegas in the same round.
