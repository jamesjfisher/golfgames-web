Skins

Every hole is worth a skin. The player with the lowest score on a hole, outright, wins it. If two or more tie for low, the skin carries over and the next hole is worth more.

Players2 or more. Everyone in the game plays everyone, hole by hole.
ScoringGross (default) or net, with an optional handicap allowance
MoneySkin worth: Free, $1, $2 or $5 a skin, from each other player

Setting it up

On the Format step, add Skins ("Win holes for skins") and open its options.

  • Who's playing: who is in the skins. Anyone left out still plays the round and keeps a full stroke play card.
  • Scoring: Gross ("Raw scores decide each skin.") or Net ("Handicap strokes are applied per hole, then the skin is decided.").
  • Handicap allowance (Net only): 100%, 90%, 80% or Custom.
  • Skin worth: whole dollars a skin. Enter 0 to play for nothing.

How it's scored

  • An outright low score wins the hole's skin, plus every skin that has carried over to it.
  • A tie for low carries the skin over: the next hole is worth one more. Carry-overs keep stacking until someone wins a hole outright.
  • Picking up: a player who picked up can't win the hole, and doesn't hold it up for anyone else. If everyone picks up, the skin carries over, as with a tie.
  • An unscored hole (anyone in the skins still has no score) decides nothing until the scores are in. It isn't a carry-over, and holes after it are still decided normally.
  • Carries left at the end: skins still carrying after the last hole aren't won by anyone, and aren't paid. The game card shows them as riding or Carrying over.

Worked example

Alex, Sam, Jordan and Casey play gross skins at Skin worth $2.

Holes 1 and 2 are tied for low, so both skins carry. Sam's birdie on hole 3 wins it outright, along with the two carried skins: 3 skins. Alex wins hole 4: 1 skin.

Each skin is worth $2 from each of the other three players:

  • Sam collects 3 × $2 × 3 = $18 and pays Alex $2, for +$16.
  • Alex collects 1 × $2 × 3 = $6 and pays Sam $6, for $0.
  • Jordan and Casey pay $6 to Sam and $2 to Alex, so −$8 each.

In the app

Partway through a round, the scorecard shows everyone's holes. Tap Skins under YOUR GAMES to see skins won and the money for each player. These screens are from a different round than the worked example above.

Settling up

For each skin, every other player in the skins pays the winner the skin's worth, and a hole that won a carry-over pays that many skins. It always balances: what the winners collect is exactly what everyone else pays. With 4 players at $2, one skin is $6 to the winner.

Joining partway through

A player added during the round is in the skins from their Skins start hole onward.

  • Back-filled holes: holes they fill in from before they joined count on their stroke play card, but not in the skins.
  • A pot that was already carrying when they joined can only be won by the players who were in when it started. The new player joins on the hole after that pot is won, and pays nothing for those holes.
