Banker

On every hole one player is the banker. The banker plays a separate one-on-one match against each of the others, all on the same hole. A banker can win one match, lose another and halve a third, all at once.

Players3 or more (up to 5, the size of a group)
ScoringLow score wins each match. A tie is a push.
MoneyThe wager: a Fixed stake of Free, $2, $5, $10 or Other a match ($5 is the default), or Table stakes.

Setting it up

On the Format step, add Banker and open its options.

  • Who's playing: three or more of the group.
  • Tee order: sets who banks first. Move players up or down, then tap Use this order.
  • Who banks the next hole:
    • In turn (the default): "One hole each, in tee order, wrapping round. Everybody banks the same number of holes, give or take one."
    • Hole winner: "Whoever wins a hole banks the next one." The lowest score on the previous hole banks. If that's tied, the group picks.
    • Until beaten: "The banker keeps the bank until somebody beats them outright." Matching the banker's score isn't enough.
    Hole 1 always goes to the first player in the tee order.
  • Scoring (Gross or Net) and Handicap allowance. In a net game each player gets strokes off their own handicap, after the allowance.
  • The wager: Fixed stake ("One amount for the whole round, set now.") or Table stakes ("The banker names it each hole, inside a range you set now."). Table stakes adds a Wager range row: $1–$5, $2–$20, $5–$50 or Custom.

Then the house rules, each a switch:

  • Blind Banker (off by default): "The banker calls it before seeing the hole, and every match on that hole is worth double."
  • Hammer (on by default): "Either side may double this hole. The other takes it, hammers back, or concedes it. Between those two players only — it ends with the hole."
  • Par 3s triple (on by default): "On a par 3 each hammer triples instead of doubling." It needs Hammer on.
  • High-Risk Banker (off by default): "The banker takes on the whole field, with nobody sitting a hole out."
  • Presses (on by default): "A challenger who is down to the banker may open a new bet running to the end of the round. The original carries on beside it." With Who can press set to Anyone it reads "Any player may open a new bet running to the end of the round. The original carries on beside it."
  • Who can press Coming in the next app update (shown while Presses is on): Whoever's down (the default), the game's own rule, or Anyone, "Anyone in the game can press once a hole has been scored, even when they are ahead. Nobody has to agree to it." The game's summary line then ends "anyone can press". See Presses.

Banker can't share a round with Four-Ball, Abilene, Wolf, 6-6-6 or Vegas.

How a hole is scored

Every challenger is in against the banker unless somebody says otherwise, so an ordinary hole needs nothing but the scores. To change a hole, tap Set up on the hole screen. The sheet shows the field, one card per challenger:

  • In: plays the banker for the hole's wager.
  • Passed: the challenger sat this hole out.
  • Not faced: the banker didn't take this challenger on.
  • Everyone in puts them all back. With High-Risk Banker on, nobody can sit a hole out.

Each live match is decided on its own: the lower score wins the wager from the other, and a tie pays nothing. A match that was passed or not faced pays nothing either.

  • Blind Banker: switch it on in the sheet before the hole, and every match on the hole is worth double.
  • Hammer: either player in a match can hammer it, doubling that match only (tripling on a par 3 with Par 3s triple on). The other player takes it, hammers back to double it again, or concedes. A conceded match pays what it was worth before the hammer that was turned down, with no scores needed. The banker's other matches on the hole are untouched.
  • Table stakes: the banker sets the wager for their hole in the sheet, inside the range.
  • Mistakes: "Calls are made before anyone tees off — but fix a mis-recorded one here whenever you spot it, on any hole."

Presses

A press is a new, separate match between a challenger and a banker, over the holes still to play. It's settled once, as a single bet, and the original matches carry on beside it. The sheet is titled Press the bet.

  • Who can press: "You can press anyone you are down to, counting every bet between you." With Who can press set to Anyone, the sheet says "Either player in a pair can press, ahead or behind. A press is a new match over the holes that are left." Nobody can press until a Banker hole has been played: "Nothing to press yet — a press needs a Banker hole on the card."
  • Which holes: from the hole after the last one anybody has scored, to the end of the round. The bank keeps rotating, and the press is still between the same two players.
  • Amount: The new bet is 1×, 2× or 3× the wager on the hole just played.
  • How it's won: hole by hole, like a match. It can end early, for example 3&2.
  • Pressing again: a second press between the same two players is another new match. A press you're winning counts towards the money between you, so it can stop you pressing that player again.

In the app

Partway through a round, the scorecard shows everyone's holes. Tap Banker under YOUR GAMES to see each player's won-lost record and money, and Press the bet. These screens are from a different round than the worked example above.

Settling up

  • Every match is between two people. The loser pays the winner that match's wager, doubled for a Blind Banker and for each hammer (tripled for each hammer on a par 3 with Par 3s triple on). Nobody can win or lose more than the matches they were in.
  • Your total is the sum of all your matches, as banker and as challenger, plus any presses.
  • A press pays its amount once, to whoever wins it.
  • At the end of the round, a hole still missing a score in a live match pays nothing. A press that hasn't been decided when the round ends pays nothing, even if someone is ahead in it.

Good to know

  • Picking up loses your match on the hole, unless the other player also picked up, which is a halve.
  • Mixed tees: whether a hole is a par 3 for the hammer is decided by the banker's own tee.
  • The banker is fixed once the hole is set. Changing the tee order mid-round doesn't change who banked the holes already played.
