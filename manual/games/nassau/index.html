Nassau

A Nassau is three matches in one: the Front 9, the Back 9 and the Overall 18. Each is settled on its own, so a slow start doesn't decide the day.

Players2 or more, playing head-to-head matches
Round length18 holes. Adding Nassau locks the round to all 18.
Scoring styleMatch Play (holes won) or Stroke (lowest total)
MoneyBet per nine: $5, $10, $20, $50 or Other, riding on each of the three bets. Presses add new bets.

Setting it up

On the Format step, add Nassau ("Front 9, Back 9 & Overall bets") and open its options.

  • Who plays who: with 2 players the match is set for you. With 3 or more, choose the pairs. One player can be in several matches, and every match plays the same amount.
  • Scoring style: Match Play ("Each segment is won hole by hole.") or Stroke ("Each segment is won by lowest total strokes."). Stroke is the default.
  • Bet per nine: rides on each of the three bets. $0 shows as Free.
  • Press the back nine (on by default): "The back nine pays double at settlement." This isn't a press: it doubles the Back 9 bet, decided before the round starts.
  • Who can press Coming in the next app update (shown once the bet per nine is more than $0): Whoever's down (the default), the game's own rule, or Anyone, "Anyone in the game can press once a hole has been scored, even when they are ahead. Nobody has to agree to it." The game's summary line then ends "anyone can press". See Presses.
  • Scoring (Gross or Net) and Handicap allowance.

How each bet is won

  • The three bets: Front 9 is holes 1–9, Back 9 is holes 10–18 (starting again at all square), and Overall is all 18.
  • Match Play style: each bet is won hole by hole. It can end early (shown like 3&2); one decided on its last hole reads 1 UP, and a level one is All Square.
  • Stroke style: the lower total over the bet's holes wins. A stroke bet can't be won early. It's decided once all its holes are played, and a tie is a push.
  • A level bet is a push and pays nothing.
  • Net Nassau: each player's handicap is first scaled by the allowance. The higher handicap then gets the difference between the two, on the hardest holes. For example, 16 and 11 at 90% become 14 and 10, a difference of 4 strokes.
In a net Nassau where the two handicaps differ, the scorecard grid shows each player net of their own handicap, while the match plays the difference. The card says so: "Net at each player's own handicap. The match plays the difference." The bets and the money always follow the match.

Presses

A press is a new, separate bet on the holes left in one of the three bets. The original bet carries on unchanged.

  • Who can press: only the player who is behind in that bet, or either player when it's level. With Who can press set to Anyone, the sheet says "Either side can press, ahead or behind, and a press is a new bet on the holes that are left." The bet still needs a hole scored and a hole left to play.
  • Which holes: it starts on the hole after the last hole you've both scored, and runs to the end of that bet. A Front 9 press ends at hole 9.
  • Amount: 1×, 2× or 3× the bet.
  • Calling one: press from the scorecard or hole screen. The sheet is titled Press the bet.
  • Pressing again: presses can stack. Once there's a press, whether you can press again depends on the latest press. A second one is labelled "Front 9 · press 2".
  • Not offered: on a bet already decided, on one with no holes left, or on a Nassau played for $0.

In the app

Partway through a round, the scorecard shows everyone's holes. Tap Nassau under YOUR GAMES to see the Front 9, Back 9 and Overall bets, what has settled so far, and Press the bet. These screens are from a different round than the worked example above.

Settling up

  • Each bet: every decided bet with a winner moves its amount from the loser to the winner. The Back 9 pays double when Press the back nine is on, and each press pays its own amount. A Nassau can cost at most 3× the bet before presses, or 4× with the back nine doubled. The setup summary says so: "$5 a nine, $15 a match."
  • During the round, only bets already decided count. An undecided one shows its standing, such as "2 UP" or "AS · thru 6".
  • When the round is completed or ended early, an undecided bet goes to whoever is leading it, and a level one is a push. Presses work the same way.
  • Several matches: a player's Nassau total adds up their own matches only.

Good to know

  • Picking up loses the hole in Match Play style. If both pick up, it's halved. In Stroke style a pick-up counts as a net double bogey.
  • There are no automatic presses. A press is always called by a player.
