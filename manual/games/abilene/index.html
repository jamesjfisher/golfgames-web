Abilene

A points game for the whole group. Every hole has a point for Prox (closest to the pin), Fewest Putts and Low Score, plus Low Total when you play in teams. Win every category on a hole and you earn the bucket 🪣, a bonus point.

Players2 to 5. Two or three play each for themselves, four play two teams of two, and five play two matches.
ScoringA point for each category won outright on a hole, plus the bucket for a sweep
MoneyStake per point: in Quarters (1, 2 or 4) or Dollars ($1, $2 or $5), or Other. 1 quarter a point is the default.

Setting it up

On the Format step, add Abilene ("Points game: Prox, Putts, Low Score") and open its options.

  • Who's playing: at least two. How many play decides the format: each for themselves with 2 or 3, teams with 4, two matches with 5.
  • Teams (4 players): choose the two teams of two.
  • 5-player teams (5 players): "Tap two players to make Team A — the fixed pair." Of the other three, one is the Shared Player, who partners each of the last two in turn. The Match Preview shows the two matches: the fixed pair against the shared player and one partner, and against the shared player and the other.
  • Stake per point: pick Quarters or Dollars, then the amount, in whole quarters or whole dollars. It can't be free.
  • Scoring (Gross or Net): "Handicap strokes apply to Low Score / Low Total only — Prox and Putts stay gross." Abilene has no handicap allowance: each player gets the strokes from their full course handicap.

Abilene can't share a round with Wolf, Banker, 6-6-6 or Vegas.

How a hole is scored

Enter every player's score and putts. On the hole screen, tap ⚑ Prox for the player closest to the pin.

  • Prox: only a player who hit the green in regulation can have it. For anyone else the button is greyed out, with "needs GIR" beside it. One player a hole, or nobody.
  • Fewest Putts: the fewest putts, alone. In teams, each team adds its two players' putts.
  • Low Score: the lowest score, alone. In teams, the point goes to the team of the player with the lowest score. If players on both teams tie for lowest, nobody gets it.
  • Low Total (teams only): each team adds its two scores, and the lower total wins.
  • Ties win nothing. A category two players or teams tie for gives no point to anyone.
  • The bucket: win every category on the hole (3 each for yourself, 4 in teams) and you get one point more.

A hole is scored once every player has a score and putts, or has picked up. With five players, each match is scored on its own, between its two teams.

Presses

A press raises the stake from a hole you choose to the end of the round. The sheet is titled Press the bet.

  • Who can press: "Only a side in last place can press, and the stake can never come back down." Last place is counted in money. A side is a player, or a team.
  • From which hole: Starts on can be any hole of the round, including one already played. Holes before it keep their old stake.
  • Amount: at least one step above the stake in force on that hole: a quarter a step in Quarters, $1 a step in Dollars.
  • Five players: a press raises one match, not both.
  • "Nobody can press yet — a press needs a hole on the card."

In the app

Partway through a round, the scorecard shows everyone's holes. Tap Abilene under YOUR GAMES to see each match, your total, and Press the bet. These screens are from a different round than the worked example above.

Settling up

  • Each pair of players, or of teams, settles the difference in their points at the stake.
  • Teams: each player on a team wins or loses the team's figure.
  • Five players: each match settles on its own. The fixed pair and the shared player play in both matches and add the two results.
  • Presses: each hole is paid at the stake in force for it.
  • At the end of the round, only holes everyone finished count.

Good to know

  • Picking up takes you out of every category on that hole. In teams, a team with a pick-up can't win Fewest Putts or Low Total there.
  • Net strokes only change Low Score and Low Total. Prox and Fewest Putts are always gross.
  • Green in regulation is worked out for you: your score minus your putts is two or more under par.
