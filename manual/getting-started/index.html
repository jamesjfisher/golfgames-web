Getting started

Create your account, then spend a minute on your profile. Your handicap is what makes net scoring work.

Create an account or sign in

When you open GolfGames you see the Sign In screen. You can sign in three ways:

  • Email and password: enter them and tap Sign In.
  • Sign in with Google.
  • Sign in with Apple (on iPhone; on Android it is shown only where Apple sign-in is available).

Google and Apple create your account the first time you use them, so there is nothing else to fill in.

To create an account with your email instead, tap Create an account under New to GolfGames? at the bottom of the screen. Enter your full name, email and a password of at least 8 characters, then tap Create Account. We email you a confirmation link. Tap it to activate the account, then sign in.

Forgot your password? Tap Forgot Password? on the Sign In screen. We email you a link to set a new one.

The home screen

After you sign in you land on Home. It shows your name, your home course and your handicap, with a Start a round button. Below it are Recent rounds and Friends activity, there from the start: each says so while it is empty, until you finish a round or add friends. Rounds in progress and friends who are playing right now appear above them.

The tabs along the bottom are Home, Rounds (your round history), Friends and Profile.

The HCP on the home screen is your handicap: your GHIN Handicap Index if you have linked GHIN, otherwise the handicap in your profile. Tap it to view GHIN, or to edit your handicap. It shows --.- until you have one.

Your profile

Tap the Profile tab. The green header shows your name, email, handicap index and how many rounds you have played. Below it are:

  • Ledger: what you owe and are owed from games with friends.
  • Edit Profile: your name, photo, handicap and Venmo username.
  • GHIN Account: link your official handicap.
  • Home Course: the course you play most.
  • Settings: notifications, privacy and your account.

Edit Profile and your handicap

In Edit Profile you can change your display name and photo. Your email cannot be changed.

Handicap is your Handicap Index, used for net scoring. Leave it blank to play off scratch. If you link GHIN, your index is kept up to date for you.

Venmo username is optional. Add it so golfers who owe you can pay you in Venmo. Only your friends can see it.

Tap Save Changes when you are done.

Link your GHIN account

If you have a GHIN account, link it and your Handicap Index updates automatically.

  1. On the Profile tab, tap GHIN Account.
  2. Enter your GHIN email, GHIN password and GHIN number.
  3. Tap Link Account.

Once linked, the screen shows your name, club and current handicap, with a button to refresh it and one to unlink.

Your GHIN email and password are stored securely on your phone only. They are used to sign in to GHIN for you and read your handicap. GolfGames never posts scores or changes anything in your GHIN account.

GolfGames is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the USGA or GHIN.

Set your home course

Tap Home Course and search by course name, city or state. Your home course appears on your home screen and helps friends see where you play most. Tap the ✕ to clear it.
