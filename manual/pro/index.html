GolfGames Pro

GolfGames is free to download and use. Pro is a subscription that lets you start rounds with games on top of stroke play.

What is free

Everything except starting a round with a Pro game is free, for everyone, including:

  • Stroke play rounds, on your own or with friends and guests, gross or net.
  • Playing in a friend's round, whatever games it has.
  • Entering scores by tap or by voice, and GPS yardages.
  • Linking GHIN and keeping your handicap up to date.
  • Live rounds, following friends and Friends activity.
  • Your full round history and stats.
  • Settling up and the ledger.

What Pro adds

With Pro you can start a round with any of these games:

  • Match Play, Skins and Four-Ball
  • Nassau, with auto-press
  • Abilene, the points game (Prox, Putts, Low Score)
  • Wolf, with a rotating Wolf and Lone Wolf calls
  • Banker, with blind calls, hammers and presses
  • 6-6-6, with partners that rotate every six holes
  • Vegas, where both partners' scores make one two-digit number
  • 9-Point (5-3-1) for threesomes, with Sweep and Birdie bonus

Only the person who starts the round needs Pro. The players they add do not.

On a free account these games show a PRO badge when you start a round. Tapping one opens the Pro screen. You can still start the round with stroke play only. See Starting a round.

Plans and prices

The Pro screen lists the plans under CHOOSE A PLAN, with the price for your country and how often it is billed. The App Store or Google Play sets the price you pay.

  • Founding Member: a yearly plan for the first 500 members, shown while places remain.
  • Annual: billed every year.
  • Monthly: billed every month.

Pick a plan and tap Continue. The purchase goes through the App Store or Google Play, and Pro is added to the GolfGames account you are signed in to.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Restoring a purchase

Pro belongs to your GolfGames account, so signing in on a new phone or after reinstalling the app brings it with you.

If you have subscribed but GolfGames does not show Pro, start a round and tap a game with a PRO badge to open the Pro screen, then tap Already subscribed? Restore. GolfGames checks the App Store or Google Play account on the phone and turns Pro on for the account you are signed in to. If there is nothing to restore, it says No Purchases Found.

Changing or cancelling

Go to Profile › Settings › Manage Subscription. It opens your subscriptions in the App Store or Google Play, where you can change plan or cancel.

Deleting your GolfGames account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play first.
