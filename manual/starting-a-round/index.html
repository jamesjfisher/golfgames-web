Starting a round

Setting up a round takes three steps: the course, the players, and the games you play on top of stroke play.

Open New Round

On Home, tap Start a round.

The green bar across the top of New Round shows the three steps: Course, Players and Format. Use Back at the bottom to go back a step.

Already have a round going? GolfGames plays one round at a time, so it asks first. Tap Continue to go back to that round. If the round is yours, Discard & Start New throws it away and ends it for anyone else in it. If you joined it from a friend, Leave & Start New leaves it. The round carries on for everyone else.

Step 1: Course

Search by course name, city or state. With the search box empty, you see your FAVORITES and NEARBY COURSES. Nearby courses need location access. Without it the screen says Enable location to see nearby courses, or search above.

Tap a course to open its page, with its tees, rating, slope and scorecard. You can look at any tee there, but it does not pick one: each player picks their own tees on the next step. Tap Play This Course to go on to Players.

Step 2: Players

The top of the step shows the course, the number of holes and par. Below it is the Players card, one row per golfer, with the columns PLAYER, HCP and TEE. You are always row 1. Under each name it says You, Friend or Guest, followed by that player's course handicap (CH) for the tees they are playing.

A round holds up to 5 players. The note under the card counts them, for example 3 of 5 players.

Add friends and guests

Tap Add player. The Who's playing? sheet lists the people you PLAYED WITH RECENTLY and all your FRIENDS, with a search box. Tap a friend to add them with the handicap from their profile.

For someone without the app, tap Add a guest. A new row opens on the card. Type the guest's name and, if you know it, their handicap, then tap Add to card. Leave the handicap blank to play them off scratch.

To take someone off the card, tap the ✕ at the end of their row.

Only friends can be added from the list. To add someone who has the app but is not your friend yet, send them a friend request from the Friends tab first, or add them as a guest.

Handicaps

The HCP column shows each player's Handicap Index. Scr means scratch. Tap a handicap to change it. The change applies to this round only: it is not saved to anyone's profile. Leave the box blank to play scratch.

Your own handicap comes from your profile, or from GHIN if you have linked it. See Getting started.

Tees

Each player can play different tees. Tap a player's tee to open their tee list. Each tee shows its rating and slope, its yardage and the course handicap that player would get from it. Tap a tee to choose it.

Tick Remember … at … at the top of the list to have that tee picked for that player the next time you play this course. When you choose the course's default tee, the box reads Use the course default for … next time.

Step 3: Format

Every round is scored as stroke play, so the GAMES heading says Stroke Play is always on. Games are optional. Each one adds something to play for on top of your score.

Choose games

Tap Add a game. The Choose a game sheet lists every game. You can play up to 4 at once. Games under PICK UP TO 4 fit your group. Games under NOT WITH THIS GROUP need a different number of players, and each says why in place of its description. Tap the ⓘ beside a game to read how it works, or see its page under Games.

Each game you pick becomes a card on the Format step. Tap the card to set it up: its options open in a sheet, and Done closes it. When a game still needs something, its card shows what in an amber note with a Set up button, and Start Round stays faded until every game is ready. Tap the ✕ on a card to remove that game.

Pro games. Starting a round with any game other than stroke play needs GolfGames Pro. On a free account those games show a PRO badge, and tapping one opens the Pro screen. Joining a friend's round is free, whatever games it plays.

Gross or net

Each game's sheet has a Scoring row, Gross or Net, and every game starts on Gross. The line under the choice says how that game uses it.

  • Gross: raw scores decide the game.
  • Net: handicap strokes are applied first. Net games also have a Handicap allowance row: 100% (shown as Full handicap), 90%, 80% or Custom.

Gross and net are set per game, so one round can play a gross game and a net game together. Your stroke play score is always shown both ways.

Sharing

Under SHARING, Share live with friends is on for every new round: Friends get a notification and can follow along.

Turn it off for a private round: Off: friends aren't notified and can't see this round until it's finished. Players in the round still see it. You choose this here, before the round starts. It cannot be changed once the round is under way.

Holes

Under HOLES, choose Front 9, Back 9 or 18. The line underneath names the holes, par and yardage you picked. Nassau is always played over all 18 holes, so with Nassau in the round the nines cannot be picked.

Start the round

Tap Start Round. The scorecard opens, ready for hole 1. Friends you added see the round on their Home screen and can open it on their own phone.
