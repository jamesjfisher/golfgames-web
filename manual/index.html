Games

Every round is scored as stroke play. Games are added on top, and you can play more than one at a time.

Stroke playThe base of every round: total strokes, gross and net. Match PlayHole-by-hole, head to head. SkinsWin a hole outright to win the skin; ties carry over. NassauThree matches in one: front nine, back nine, and overall. Four-BallTwo-person teams, best ball on each hole.
