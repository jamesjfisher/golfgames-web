After the round

Every round you save goes into your history, with its scorecard, its games and your stats.

The round summary

The summary is where a round is reviewed and saved. Open it from the scorecard with Summary or View Summary. It shows:

  • Your score and score to par, with your playing partners' scores beside it.
  • Fairways, GIR, Putts and your Net score.
  • A card for each game, with its result and who pays whom.
  • The Scorecard and your Highlights, such as your best hole.
  • In a group with no games, My Group ranks everyone's scores.

Tap a playing partner's score to see the round from their side. This changes only what you are looking at.

To save the round, tap End Round. See Ending or leaving a round for what each button does, and what happens to any amounts the games leave between players.

Round Rating

For an 18-hole round with every hole scored, the summary includes a Round Rating when you have a handicap. It estimates how often a golfer with your handicap would shoot your score or better, such as 1 in 40 rounds, and labels the round from Typical Round up to Outlier / Career Round. Tap it to see how it is worked out.

The Round Rating describes one round. It does not change your handicap.

Your round history

The Rounds tab (My Rounds) keeps every round you have saved. The banner at the top shows ROUNDS, your handicap (HCP), your average score (AVG) and your best score (BEST). The average and best come from your recent complete 18-hole rounds.

If you have a round going, it is pinned at the top: tap Continue to go back to it.

The tab has three views:

  • History: your rounds, newest first, grouped by month. Each row shows your score, score to par, the course, the date, the games you played, and what you won or lost in them.
  • Stats: your scoring average and trend, fairways, greens, putts per round, scrambling, 3-putts, bounce-backs, scoring by par, and the mix of birdies, pars and bogeys. Filter it to one course or all courses.
  • Courses: every course you have played. Tap one to see how you score there, hole by hole.

Round details

Tap a round in History to open it. It shows the same summary you saw when the round ended: scores, games, scorecard and highlights.

To delete a round from your history, swipe its row to the left and tap Delete. GolfGames asks you to confirm first.

Your handicap

GolfGames does not calculate a Handicap Index from your rounds. The HCP shown on Home, on the Rounds tab and on your profile is the handicap you entered in your profile or, if you have linked GHIN, your GHIN Handicap Index, kept up to date for you. See Getting started.

GolfGames never posts scores to GHIN. To count a round toward your official handicap, post it in GHIN yourself.
