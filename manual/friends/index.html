Friends

Friends can add each other to rounds, follow each other's live rounds, and keep a ledger of what is owed.

The Friends tab

The Friends tab has a search box at the top and a strip with three counts: FRIENDS, PLAYING (friends on the course right now) and REQUESTS (requests waiting for you to answer). Below them come, in order:

  • REQUESTS: friend requests sent to you.
  • SENT: requests you have sent that are still waiting.
  • PEOPLE YOU MAY KNOW: friends of your friends.
  • FRIENDS: everyone you are friends with. A friend who is playing right now is listed first.

A section with nothing in it is not shown. Pull down to refresh the tab.

Finding friends

Type at least two letters into Find friends by name or email. GolfGames searches names, usernames and email addresses, and shows the closest matches. An email address is used only to find someone. It is never shown in the results.

Tap Add to send a friend request. The button changes to Pending until they answer. Someone who is already your friend shows Friends.

A friend needs a GolfGames account. To play with someone who does not have one, add them to your round as a guest. See Add friends and guests.

People you may know

PEOPLE YOU MAY KNOW lists people who are friends with your friends, with the ones you share the most friends with first. Each row says who links you, for example Friends with Rick, Ted and Bill. Tap Add to send a request, or See all for the full list.

Friend requests

A request sent to you appears under REQUESTS. Tap Accept to become friends, or Decline to turn it down.

A request you sent appears under SENT until it is answered. Tap Cancel to withdraw it.

A friend's profile

Tap a friend to open their profile. It shows their handicap (HCP), how many rounds they have played (ROUNDS) and their best score (BEST), then:

  • Their friends, with Add beside anyone you are not friends with yet. This section is not shown if they have turned off Show my friends to friends in Settings.
  • RECENT ROUNDS: their finished rounds. Tap one to see its scorecard and games.

You see the scores and results of a friend's round, but not the amounts in its games, unless you played in it.

Removing a friend

On the Friends tab, press and hold the friend's row, then tap Remove. Removing a friend does not clear anything on your ledger with them, and they can no longer see your Venmo username.

Round invites Coming in the next app update

When a friend adds you to a round, Home shows a ROUND INVITE card with Accept and Decline. See Playing in a friend's round.

To let a friend add you without asking, open their profile and turn on Sam can add me without asking. Rounds with Sam are then saved to your account straight away. Tapping Always accept from Sam on an invite turns the same switch on. Turn it off at any time; a round you decline stays declined either way.

The same goes the other way: a friend you add to your round sees an invite. See Who has accepted.

Once round invites are fully switched on: until you accept a friend's round, nothing from it is saved to your account or your ledger, and a friend you add who has not accepted by the end plays as a guest. Until then, rounds are saved and settled for every friend in them, as before.

Following a live round

When a friend is on the course, their row on the Friends tab shows the course, the hole they are on and their score to par, with a LIVE badge. On Home, their round is under FRIENDS PLAYING, along with any of your other friends in the same group.

Tap either one to open the Live Round screen. It shows the scorecard and the games as they are played, and updates as scores come in. Pull down to refresh it. Following a round is read-only: you cannot enter or change scores, and you do not see the amounts in its games.

Depending on your notification settings, you may also get a notification when a friend starts or finishes a round.

If a friend adds you to their round, it is not a round to follow: it appears on your Home screen under ROUND IN PROGRESS, ready to play. See Playing in a friend's round.

Friends activity

The FRIENDS ACTIVITY section on Home lists your friends' finished rounds, for example finished 18 at Cedar Ridge GC, with their score. Tap a round to see its scorecard.

Before you have any friends it shows No friends yet with a Find friends link to the Friends tab.

Private rounds

A round started with Share live with friends turned off is private. Your friends are not notified when you start or finish it, and they cannot follow it while you play. Once it is finished, it appears in their Friends activity like any other round. See Live rounds and private rounds.

Who looked at your round

When a friend opens your round's scorecard, while you play or after you finish, you can see that they looked. Friends who are playing in the round with you are not counted, and opening the same round again adds to that friend's count rather than listing them twice.

  • While you play. Once someone has looked, a chip appears in the green banner of your scorecard: 2 watching while friends have it open right now, otherwise 3 looked. Tap it to open Watching your round, which lists them under WATCHING NOW and LOOKED EARLIER, for example from hole 9 · 2 times or Looked at 1:38 PM · hole 7.
  • After the round. The finished round's details have a Viewed by card listing each friend who looked, for example Live from hole 7 · last Nov 9, 8:05 AM for a friend who followed it live, or Looked Nov 9, 8:05 AM for one who opened it afterwards.

Only you see who looked at your round. When you open a friend's round, a line under the header says so, for example Sam Brooks can see that you looked. To look without being listed, turn off Show when I view rounds in Settings; see Privacy.

Who sees what

  • Only your friends can follow your live rounds. Your finished rounds can be seen by your friends and by the people who played in them.
  • Only your friends can see your Venmo username.
  • Your friends can see who your friends are, unless you turn off Show my friends to friends in Settings. Turning it off hides your list. It does not take you off other people's lists.
  • Only the round's owner and the players in it can see whether you accepted or declined a round invite. They can also tell whether you let them add you without asking.
  • When you open a friend's round, they can see that you looked, unless you turn off Show when I view rounds in Settings. See Who looked at your round.
