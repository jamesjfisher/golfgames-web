Playing a round

Enter scores hole by hole, keep an eye on your games, and end the round when you are done. Everyone in the round sees the same card.

The scorecard

The scorecard opens when the round starts. At the top, a green banner shows the course and your tees, your score, the games you are playing and how many holes you are through. Below it:

  • YOUR GAMES: a tile for each game, with where it stands and what you are up or down. Tap a tile to open that game. The Gross / Net switch on this heading changes the scores shown on the card.
  • The card itself: every player's score on every hole. Tap a hole to open it and enter or change scores.

At the bottom, Continue - Hole … takes you to the next hole to score, and Summary shows the round so far, with every game's result. Once every hole is scored, the button reads View Summary. Pull the card down to refresh it.

Entering scores

Each hole has its own screen. The green banner at the top shows the hole number, its par and yardage, and the hole's stroke index (HCP). In a group there is one card per player.

  • Score: tap the number of strokes. The number for par is marked so you can find it quickly.
  • Putts and Fairway (hit or missed) are optional, for your stats.
  • GIR (green in regulation) is worked out for you from the score and putts.

Tap Next Hole to save and move on. On the last hole the button reads Round Summary. The button on the left goes back a hole.

Picking up

If a player picks up without holing out, tap ✕ Picked up. What happens next depends on the games in the round:

  • When no game in the round counts the hole, the card says No score for this hole · no putts or GIR recorded. Stroke play leaves the hole out of the total.
  • When a game keeps a running total, the card shows Scores … with a number, net double bogey · no putts or GIR recorded. That game counts the hole as a net double bogey.

To undo it, tap a number.

Entering scores by voice

Tap the microphone in the green banner, on the scorecard or on a hole, and say the score. For example, say "bogey, two putts, hit the fairway". In a group, say each player's first name and their score, such as "Alex 5, Sam 4". A two-digit number is strokes then putts, so "Alex 42" means 4 strokes and 2 putts. GolfGames shows what it heard: tap Apply to enter it, or Cancel.

On the scorecard you can name the hole too, for example "Hole 7, Alex 42, Sam 53". Voice entry needs microphone access, which your phone asks for the first time you use it.

GPS yardages

On a hole's screen, tap GPS at the top right to see how far you are from the green. The big number is the middle of the green, with the front and back underneath.

GolfGames asks for location access the first time. It uses your location only while the Yardages screen is open, and never saves or shares it. The distances appear once your phone has an accurate fix: if it takes a while, step out from under trees, or tap Restart GPS.

Not every course has yardages yet. When the course you are playing has none, the GPS button is grey, and tapping it says Yardages aren't available for this course yet. If the distances look wrong, tap Distances look wrong? to report the hole.

Live rounds and private rounds

A round with Share live with friends on (the default) is live: your friends can get a notification when you start, and they see the round on their Home screen under FRIENDS PLAYING. Tapping it opens a read-only scorecard that updates as you play.

A private round is one started with that switch off. Friends are not notified and do not see it until it is finished. Everyone playing in it sees it as normal. You choose this when you start the round, and it cannot be changed during the round.

Playing in a friend's round

When a friend adds you to their round, your Home screen shows a ROUND INVITE card: Sam added you to a round, with the course. Choose:

  • Accept: the round opens on your phone, and from then on it appears on Home under ROUND IN PROGRESS, with your friend's name. Tap Resume round → to open it again.
  • Decline: the round leaves your Home screen.
  • Always accept from Sam: accepts this round, and Sam can add you to rounds from now on without asking. You can turn this off on Sam's profile; see Round invites.

A round from a friend you always accept from skips the card and goes straight under ROUND IN PROGRESS.

Until you accept, nothing from the round is saved to your account. If you decline, you play in it as a guest: your card and any money stay on that round, and nothing from it is saved to your account or your ledger. If you accept while offline, you play as a guest until you are back online.

Anyone in the round can enter scores, for any player, and they reach everyone's phone. You can only be in one round at a time: if you already have a round of your own going, finish it or abandon it first.

Who has accepted

When your round has friends in it, each player's card on the hole screen, and their name on the scorecard, shows where they stand: You, Invited (dashed: they have not answered yet), Accepted, or Declined. Guests, and players who are not your friend, show nothing. It updates as your friends answer. A friend you add during the round is invited the same way.

Keep entering every player's scores as usual. Today the round is saved and settled for every friend in it, whether or not they have answered.

A decline shows as Declined · guest on the hole screen and Guest on the scorecard. While someone is still invited, a note under the cards says so, for example Sam's scores are held on this round. They're saved to Sam's account only if Sam accepts before the round ends. A friend who has not accepted when the round ends plays as a guest: their card and money stay on the round, and nothing is saved to their account or ledger. The round summary warns you before you save, and the finished round's details say who played as a guest. See Settling up with a friend who did not accept.

Adding a player during the round

If someone joins you on the course, whoever started the round can add them. Under the card, tap Add player. It says which hole they would join on. Pick a friend, or Add a guest, then set:

  • Starts on: the first hole they play. It starts on the hole you are on.
  • For the holes before that, Didn't play leaves them blank. Enter scores lets you fill them in afterwards, on the card.
  • Tees and Handicap.
  • Games: a new player plays stroke play on their own card. If the round has Skins, a Skins switch lets them play for skins from the hole they start on. Skins already won stay won.

Tap Add … to the round. A round still holds up to 5 players.

Ending or leaving a round

Open Summary from the scorecard. At the bottom are your ways out. Continue Playing goes back to the card, for example to fix a score.

  • End Round saves the round to your history. It ends the round for everyone in it, not just for you.
  • Abandon Round (in a round you started) throws the round away. Nothing is saved, and it ends the round for everyone in it.
  • Leave Round (in a friend's round, in place of Abandon) takes you out and leaves the round going for everyone else. Your card is saved to your history, as long as it has 9 holes scored.

A round needs at least 9 holes scored to be saved. If you end it sooner, GolfGames warns you that it won't be saved, and you can tap Keep Playing.

When there is money in the games

If your games leave an amount between you and another player, End Round first opens Settle up before saving: Mark what changed hands on the course. For each amount choose Paid, if it was settled on the course, or On the tab, which puts it on your ledger. Then tap Save Round.

Abandoning a round like that asks what happens to the amounts. Call off the bets means nobody owes anything. Bets stand through hole … settles up on the holes played.
