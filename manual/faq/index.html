FAQ & troubleshooting

Short answers to common questions, each with a link to the full explanation.

Rounds

Do my friends need Pro to play in my round?

No. Only the person who starts a round needs Pro, and only if it has a game other than stroke play. Joining a friend's round is free, whatever games it plays. See What Pro adds.

Can I add someone who doesn't have the app?

Yes. Add them as a guest: tap Add player, then Add a guest, and type their name and handicap. See Add friends and guests.

Why can't I find someone in the Who's playing? list?

The list shows only your friends. Send them a friend request from the Friends tab first, or add them as a guest. See Finding friends.

Why does New Round ask about a round I already have going?

GolfGames plays one round at a time. You can go back to that round, or discard it (or leave it, if it is a friend's) and start a new one. See Open New Round.

How do I change a score I entered?

Tap the hole on the scorecard to open it, then tap the right number. Anyone in the round can enter or change scores for any player. See The scorecard.

Why is the GPS button grey?

The course you are playing does not have yardages yet. See GPS yardages.

Why wasn't my round saved?

A round needs at least 9 holes scored to be saved. Abandon Round never saves. A friend's round is saved to your account only if you accepted it before it ended; see Playing in a friend's round. See Ending or leaving a round.

If I end the round, does it end for everyone?

Yes. End Round and Abandon Round end the round for everyone in it. In a friend's round, Leave Round takes only you out. See Ending or leaving a round.

Handicap

Does GolfGames calculate my handicap?

No. The HCP it shows is the handicap in your profile or, if you have linked GHIN, your GHIN Handicap Index. See Your handicap.

Does GolfGames post my scores to GHIN?

No. To count a round toward your official handicap, post it in GHIN yourself. See Link your GHIN account.

Friends and privacy

Can I play without my friends seeing my round?

Yes. Turn off Share live with friends when you start the round. Friends are not notified and cannot follow it until it is finished. See Sharing.

Can my friends see how much I won or lost?

Not unless they played in the round. Friends who follow or look at a round see the scores and results, but no amounts. See Who sees what.

How do I turn off notifications?

In your phone's settings for GolfGames. See Notifications.

Settling up

Why is a payment I recorded still pending?

A payment with another golfer counts once they confirm it. Until then it shows as waiting on them. See Confirming a payment.

Why doesn't Venmo fill in the golfer I'm paying?

Venmo fills them in only when they have added a Venmo username and you are friends. Otherwise pick them in Venmo yourself. A guest's tab offers cash only. See Paying in Venmo.

Does money go through GolfGames?

No. You pay in cash or in Venmo, and GolfGames keeps the record. See Settle up & ledger.

Account and Pro

I forgot my password.

Tap Forgot Password? on the Sign In screen, and we email you a link to set a new one. See Create an account or sign in.

I subscribed, but GolfGames doesn't show Pro.

Use Already subscribed? Restore on the Pro screen. See Restoring a purchase.

How do I cancel Pro?

In the App Store or Google Play, through Profile › Settings › Manage Subscription. See Changing or cancelling.

How do I delete my account?

Profile › Settings › Delete Account. Cancel any Pro subscription first. See Deleting your account.

Still stuck?

Email support@golfgames.gg.
