Settings & account

Your profile, privacy, notifications, your subscription, signing out and deleting your account.

The Profile tab

Everything about your account starts on the Profile tab. Under your name and email are:

  • Ledger: what you owe and are owed. See Your ledger.
  • Edit Profile: your name, photo, handicap and Venmo username. See Edit Profile and your handicap.
  • GHIN Account: link or unlink GHIN. See Link your GHIN account.
  • Home Course: see Set your home course.
  • Settings: the screen described below.

To change your photo, tap Change Photo in Edit Profile and choose Take Photo or Choose from Library. Once you have a photo, Remove Photo takes it off. Your email cannot be changed.

The Settings screen

Tap Profile › Settings. It has six sections:

  • SUBSCRIPTION: Manage Subscription opens your subscriptions in the App Store or Google Play. See Changing or cancelling.
  • PRIVACY: Show my friends to friends, described below.
  • NOTIFICATIONS: Notifications opens your notification switches. See Notifications.
  • HELP: User manual opens this manual in your browser.
  • LEGAL: the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • ACCOUNT: Delete Account. See Deleting your account.

Privacy

Show my friends to friends is on unless you turn it off. While it is on, your friends can see your friends list on your profile, and your friends can be suggested to one another under PEOPLE YOU MAY KNOW through you. Turning it off hides your list and stops anyone being suggested through you. It does not take you off other people's lists.

Other things that decide who sees what:

  • Private rounds. Turn off Share live with friends when you start a round, and friends are not notified and cannot follow it until it is finished. See Sharing.
  • Amounts. Only the players in a round see the amounts in its games. See Who pays whom.
  • Venmo username. Only your friends can see it, and only while you are friends.
  • Email. Someone can find you by searching your email address, but it is never shown to them.

For the full list, see Who sees what.

Notifications

GolfGames sends two kinds of notification:

  • Friends' rounds: when a friend starts a round (for example Alex Parker started a round) and when they finish one. You are not notified about a round you are playing in, or about a friend's private round.
  • Payments: when another golfer records a payment with you, asking you to confirm it. See Confirming a payment.

To choose which you get, tap Profile › Settings › Notifications. It has a switch for each, both on unless you turn them off:

  • Friend Activity: When a friend starts or finishes a round.
  • Payments: When a golfer records a payment with you.

The card at the top says how many are on, for example 2 of 2 notifications enabled. Your choice is saved to your account, so it follows you to another phone.

Your phone asks whether GolfGames may send notifications at all, and its answer overrides both switches. If notifications are off there, the Notifications screen says Notifications are turned off. with an Open Settings button. To change your answer, or to turn every GolfGames notification off, go to your phone's settings: on iPhone, Settings › Notifications › GolfGames; on Android, open the notification settings for GolfGames in your phone's app settings.

Signing out

At the bottom of the Profile tab, tap Sign Out, then Sign Out again to confirm.

Deleting your account

Tap Profile › Settings › Delete Account. GolfGames asks twice before deleting anything: tap Delete Account, then Delete. Your account is deleted with all your data, including rounds, scores, friends, activity and your profile, and you are signed out. This cannot be undone.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play first. See Changing or cancelling.

If you cannot sign in to the app, you can ask us to delete your account by email instead. See Delete Your Account.
